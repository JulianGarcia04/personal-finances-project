# Savings goals

- **Virtual goal:** Track progress without selecting a funding account. Recording a contribution only increases the goal’s saved amount. It does not create a transaction or change a bank balance. Existing unlinked goals keep this behavior without a migration.
- **Account-linked goal:** Choose an eligible account or pocket in the goal’s currency and workspace. Its balance is the goal’s progress; spending or withdrawing from that account also changes the displayed progress. A contribution moves money from another eligible account as an internal transfer. No extra expense or goal balance is recorded.
- Credit cards and bridge accounts cannot fund or back a goal. An unavailable linked account is not treated as a virtual goal. Deleting the goal does not move money.

A goal is a view of savings, not an additional asset: do not add its progress on top of account balances when calculating net worth.
