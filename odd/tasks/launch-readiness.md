# Launch readiness (phase 1: launch blockers before billing)
Close the non-billing launch blockers found in the SaaS readiness review so Vault can open a private beta.
Branch: `feat/launch-readiness` → `main` · Delivery: ask-on-risk; no push, merge or deploy without the user's request · Runner: `pnpm test` (node:test), `pnpm build` (vue-tsc + vite).

## Specs

Source: the assistant's roadmap step 1, "Bloqueantes sin cobro: legal, login, borrar y exportar datos, analítica, landing con lista de espera", accepted by the user with "okey prosigamos" (L2).

- **S1 Password recovery.** Roadmap gap: "No hay entrada con Google ni recuperación de contraseña." The login screen offers password recovery by email (Firebase `sendPasswordResetEmail`). The response does not reveal whether the email is registered.
- **S2 Google sign-in.** Same roadmap gap as S1. Login and signup offer "Continuar con Google". A first Google sign-in creates the same personal workspace and profile that email signup creates; later sign-ins reuse them.
- **S3 Legal consent.** Roadmap gap: "la Ley 1581 de 2012 (habeas data) exige política de tratamiento de datos, autorización del usuario y términos de uso." Public pages for the data-processing policy and the terms of use. Signup requires explicit acceptance, and the profile records the accepted document versions with a server timestamp. Assumption: the data controller's identity (legal name, ID number, address, contact email) stays as clearly marked placeholders until the user provides it; the text needs legal review before public launch.
- **S4 Data export.** Roadmap gap: "También faltan borrar la cuenta y exportar los datos." A signed-in user can download the active workspace's data from Settings.
- **S5 Account deletion.** Same roadmap gap as S4. Blocked: product decision on what happens to shared workspaces the user owns or belongs to.
- **S6 Measurement.** Roadmap gap: "no hay analítica ni monitoreo de errores." Blocked: provider choice and accounts.
- **S7 Landing.** Roadmap gap: "el router solo tiene /login y la app. No hay página pública ni página de precios." First customer (L7): "el primer cliente son una pareja que comparten gastos... y otra persona sola (yo)". A public landing speaks to couples who share expenses and to individuals, states that the beta is free, and links to signup, login and both legal documents. Signed-out visits to `/` show it; the installed app (standalone) keeps opening the login, and every other protected route keeps redirecting to the login. Assumption: signup ("Crear cuenta gratis") replaces the roadmap's waitlist, because signup is already open and free during the beta, and a waitlist would need public Firestore writes. Link previews get Open Graph tags. Superseded at the S8 cutover: the static site on the landing domain shows the landing, and the app's signed-out visits open the login.
- **S8 Static landing (Astro).** User (L8): "creo que la landing va a tocas hacerla con astro o nuxt (mismo diseño) para tener un buen SEO y pocisionamiento"; Astro accepted with "si" (L9). A `landing/` workspace package builds the landing and both legal documents as static HTML with no client JavaScript, reusing the same Vue components and design tokens as the app, with per-page title, description, Open Graph tags, canonical URL, sitemap and robots.txt. Domain (L14): "usemos finances.lynor.cloud"; `finanzas.lynor.cloud` redirects to it (L13: "yo digo que finances y finanzas. podemos usar ambos subdominios"). Assumptions: the app moves to `https://app.finances.lynor.cloud`, where signed-out visits open the login and the SPA `/bienvenida` is removed; builds use these production origins unless `SITE_URL` / `PUBLIC_APP_URL` override them; the landing is a second Firebase Hosting site (target `landing`, clean URLs, no trailing slash) next to the app's (target `app`). Creating that site, connecting the domains, the DNS records in Vercel, Auth authorized domains and the deploy are remote operations the user runs or authorizes.
- **S9 Product name.** User (L17): "Lynor Finanzas". It replaces "Vault" wherever people see the product name: landing, legal pages, app screens, page titles, PWA manifest, push notifications, emails, the assistant's prompt and the notes on settlement adjustments, plus the export file name. Assumption: internal identifiers stay (Firebase project IDs, storage keys, the export `format` id, package names), so no stored data or contract changes.
- **S10 Brand identity.** User (L18): "esta es la propuesta si puedes mejorar y adaptarlo a ello", with the image `Lynor Brand System Guide.png` (an "L" symbol of two leaves with a blue-to-violet gradient, the "Lynor" wordmark, app icons on white and on navy, palette, typography "Sora", and product lockups where only the descriptor and its accent color change). The Lynor symbol replaces the "V" monogram in the landing, login and sidebar, with "Finanzas" as the descriptor in the app's green accent; favicon and PWA icons use the symbol; Sora replaces Outfit as the display font. Assumptions: the descriptor stays "Finanzas" (L17) instead of the image's "Finance"; the app keeps its dark theme and its existing accent colors, because the guide changes only the symbol, the descriptor and the typography per product; improvements over the proposal: both leaves are the same shape mirrored across the cut, with one even gap, and the palette uses the colors actually shown in the swatches.
