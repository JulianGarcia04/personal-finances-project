## Specs

- **S1**: Each expense belongs to one operating budget. Its category remains independent.
- **S2**: Monthly budgets use existing calendar-month installment allocation from the transaction month.
- **S3**: Event/project totals count the full assigned purchase principal once, including early bookings.
- **S4**: Global estimated monthly allocations include every expense budget, including archived budgets; never add event committed totals to installments.
- **S5**: Preserve a protected default monthly budget backed by authoritative legacy `categoryBudgets`.
- **S6**: Legacy, unassigned, imported, and AI-created expenses resolve to that default without bulk rewriting history. Additional user-selected offline coverage: "runtime coverage for import/AI producer paths".
- **S7**: Create, edit, and archive additional budgets. Archive preserves assignments, history, and remaining obligations.
- **S8**: Budget-only reassignment changes classification, never balances, debt, dates, receipts, installments, or mirror links.
- **S9**: Reassignment restates past and future budget attribution; it does not restate financial totals.
- **S10**: Validate same-workspace budget references even for members of multiple workspaces.
- **S11**: Keep goal funding, `budgetAllocations`, the 50/30/20 planner, and existing global expense limits separate.
- **S12**: Use clearly labeled reporting currency and current manual FX semantics; flag missing rates and do not silently relabel stored caps.
- **S13 — Confirm legacy cap units once**: User selected exactly "Confirmarla una vez por espacio (recomendado)" (`confirm-legacy-currency-once`). Accepted description: "Cada espacio confirma la moneda real de sus límites. Se conserva aunque cambie la moneda de visualización; no se convierten importes." Checks: explicit workspace confirmation, scalar-only/no-conversion patch, stable denomination after reporting-currency changes, idempotent same-unit confirmation and rejected conflicting-unit confirmation with unchanged data/writes. Additional user-selected validation: "rejecting malformed stored cap-currency values"; malformed uses the authoritative `/^[A-Z]{3}$/` schema shape, not a seven-code UI allowlist.

## Acceptance examples

- A synthetic 900-unit purchase in three installments assigned to an event shows 900 committed and approximately 300 in each applicable global month; full credit debt is unchanged.
- An event assignment removes that purchase from recurring limits without changing its category or global monthly allocation.
- Monthly budgets include only their own expenses; unassigned legacy expenses remain in the protected baseline.
- Event bookings before the planned event date are retained.
- Archiving keeps history and remaining installment allocations visible.
- Reassignment never invokes balance reversal/reapplication or changes mirror relationships.
- Cross-workspace assignments fail even when the actor belongs to both workspaces.
- Card repayment transfers add no second expense cost.
- Budget/FX edits retain goal progress, planner settings, and legacy category limits.
