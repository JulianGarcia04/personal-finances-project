# Budget assignment UX
Objective: let users assign expenses and income to budgets at creation, link accounts to budgets, fund event budgets with income (model B), and move existing movements into a budget.
Branch: `feat/budget-assignment-ux` · Delivery: single-pr with size exception (user, 2026-10-22: "todo en un solo PR con size-exception") · Runner: `node --test` (`pnpm test`), functions `pnpm test:functions`, rules `pnpm test:budgets:security`.

## Specs
- S1: Creating an expense or income (`Transactions.vue` and the goal contribution in `Goals.vue`) shows a "Presupuesto" selector with active budgets plus the default monthly budget; saved through `addTransaction({ …, budgetId })`. User: "pienso que tambien los ingresos deberian estar ligado a un presupuesto".
- S2: A budget has `linkedAccountIds`. A transaction from a linked account belongs to that budget: the selector is locked to it at creation (Transactions and goal contribution), and reassignment only offers that budget. User (2026-10-22): "si una cuenta ya está ligada a un presupuesto, yo no podria registrar un gasto o ingreso a otro presupuesto y que se guarde esa cuenta". An account belongs to at most one active budget; linking it to another warns. Server enforcement (user, 2026-10-22: "hagamoslo cumplir en el servidor"): the account stores `linkedBudgetId` (kept in sync with the budget's `linkedAccountIds`); Firestore rules and server writers require an expense or income of an account linked to an active budget to carry that budget; archiving the budget frees its accounts. User: "si yo relaciono la cuenta con el presupuesto, entonces las transacciones que yo haga esa cuenta no se liga a ese presupuesto también (por herencia) ?"
- S3: Creating or editing an event budget offers "Mover N movimientos existentes": default-monthly expenses and income from linked accounts or capped categories, within the date range when set; preview with per-row uncheck; applied as budgetId-only updates in batches.
- S4: Microcopy in the budget form and card: "Los movimientos entran a este presupuesto cuando se lo asignas o cuando salen de una cuenta ligada".
- S5: Income with a budget is excluded from the monthly income in Budgets ("Ingreso real del mes") and from the alert income reference (`functions/src/budgetAlerts.ts`). Expenses of a self-funded budget (one with any assigned income) are excluded from the month's spending in both places; budgets without assigned income still count. User (2026-10-22): "sí, aplícalo así".
- S6 (model B): funds = linked income plus transfers into the budget's linked accounts minus transfers out (user, 2026-10-22: "sí, que las transferencias cuenten como fondos" / "sí, impleméntalo con esa tabla": unlinked→linked +, linked→unlinked −, same budget 0, budget A→budget B −A +B; direction from the accounts, never the stored sign); available = funds − linked expenses; the cap stays optional and independent; budgets without linked income look as today; the card shows funds and cap bars when both exist. User: "sí, vamos con la B".
- S7: Funds alert at 80 % and 100 % by email and push, reusing the existing threshold ledger.
- S8: Every new surface must have good UI and UX, consistent with the existing glass/mobile design (BaseSheet, `.field`, `.tap`). User: "A su vez recuerda que uno de los requisitos es que tenga buen UI y buen UX"
- Out of scope: the AI agent (`chatWithAgent`) does not apply links.
- S9: Dashboard and AI agent offer both views. User (2026-10-22): "quiero se apliquen las dos porque hay veces que yo voy a querer ver los ingresos totales independientemente del presupuesto y hay veces que lo quiero ver separado por presupuesto". Dashboard: toggle "Todo" (default, today's totals) / "Por presupuesto" (month income without budget funds, spending without self-funded budgets, plus per-budget breakdown: funds, spent, available), remembered per device. Agent: the summary returns total figures and a per-budget breakdown, and the agent prompt explains the difference.
